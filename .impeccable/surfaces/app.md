## Direction contract

THESIS: Boardhive is the scoring track made digital, not a dashboard bolted onto a database — progress, history, and standing are positions along a track, not cards in a feed. Refuses the generic SaaS-dashboard-with-stat-tiles arrangement this category always ships.

OWN-WORLD: Warm parchment/board-stock ground (not sterile white, not near-black). A small set of saturated "player token" colors, used only functionally (whose play, whose win) — never decorative gradients. Numerals-forward grotesk with tabular figures for scores/counts. Rounded token chips (player avatars) sit ON tracks; pips mark counts; a marker/pawn motif signals "you are here" / current state. Confident flat color fields behind numeric badges (raised from the dealt anime-title-card challenger). A persistent "flag where you left off" bookmark motif for last-viewed/current state (raised from the dealt film-cutting-bench challenger).

STORY: A hive's play history reads as a track of logged sessions. Group stats are literally track positions — the leaderboard is who's furthest along. Joining/creating a hive, logging a play, and winning are all track-advancement moments the interface dramatizes rather than reporting flatly.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The hive home shows the group's track — recent plays as beads along a line, leading players' token chips visible at a glance at the top — not a generic card grid or stat-tile row. The primary action (log a play) sits as a clear advancing move on that track, not a floating FAB with no relationship to the content.

FORM: Assigned index 3 of the builder's own ranked 7-candidate list (scorepad, player mat, convention badge/lanyard, victory-point track, rulebook iconography key, card-sleeve/deck-box shelf, felt game table), drawn from the board-game world itself rather than a foreign domain. Seed key 2d15b97a (mode: persuade, source: api). Weighed against 6 dealt challengers (antialiased racing league livery, bebop anime title card, ebru floated-pigment fluid art, film cutting-bench select rail, architectural daylight section, silk canopy color-chords) on audience identification and product clarity: won against all on audience identification (none native to board gaming); the film cutting-bench challenger was competitive on product clarity (a rail-with-marker structure close to what a track UI needs) and is not spent — its persistent-bookmark discipline is donated in above. No image generation available in this environment: build is code-led per contract, no mockup/comp round.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
